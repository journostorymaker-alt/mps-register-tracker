How this works

  • Data comes from the official House of Commons register API (interests-api.parliament.uk). A scheduled job checks for a new register twice a week and rebuilds this page.
  • Why some entries are years old: the register lists what is live, not what is new. One-off gifts, donations, trips and payments drop off 12 months after they are registered. Ongoing roles stay on for as long as they last, so a job started in 2015 can still be listed.
  • Running totals: because old items drop off, the job also downloads every past entry, expired ones included. Each entry is counted once.
  • One-off items are counted on the date received. If there's no date, the date registered is used.
  • Regular payments (monthly, quarterly or yearly) are spread across the months the agreement ran since the election, up to its end date or the latest register. This is an estimate. Some MPs register a value that covers part of a year, or update an agreement with a new entry. Check big regular payments against the source.
  • Where an agency pays the MP for someone else, the true payer is shown.
  • Payer names are tidied so spelling variants count as one. Two merges are judgement calls: the Sainsbury name variants, and Fran Perrin with Francesca Perrin.
  • Sectors are a rough keyword sort, set in scripts/sectors.py. Treat them as a guide.
  • "To charity" means the MP says the money went to charity. It is still counted in totals.
